# Operator Guide — Voice Booking Agent

## Dashboard Overview
Access your dashboard at `https://your-dashboard-url.com`.

### Managing Bookings
- **Overview**: View tomorrow's schedule at a glance.
- **Bookings Tab**: Search for customers by phone or name. You can see the status of every appointment.
- **Cancellation**: Customers can cancel via the IVR or the link sent in their confirmation SMS.

### Managing Services & Pricing
1. Navigate to **Services**.
2. Click **Edit** on any service.
3. Update the **Min/Max price**. This will immediately update what the AI tells customers over the phone.
4. Update the **Duration**. This affects which Calendly slots are offered.
5. Toggle **Active** to temporarily disable a service in the IVR.

### Handling Callbacks
If a customer selects "Leave a message" or "Speak to an operator", their request appears in the **Callbacks** tab. 
- Review the reason they provided.
- Call them back.
- Mark as "Resolved" once handled.

### Daily Summaries
Every day at 4:30 PM, you will receive an email summarizing tomorrow's schedule and estimated revenue. Ensure your email is correct in the **Settings** tab.
